Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Where we deliver

  • Benin: Cotonou, Calavi, Porto-Novo and surrounding areas — 6 to 14 business days.
  • Nigeria: Lagos Mainland, Lagos Island and other states — confirmed at checkout.
  • Neighbouring states: Lomé and selected West African destinations.

Transport fare

Fares depend on your delivery city and the weight of the parcel. The exact fare is confirmed with you after your order is placed, before dispatch.

Tracking

Once your order is confirmed you will receive an order ID. Use it on our Track order page, or message us on WhatsApp for a status update.

Delivery issues

If a parcel is delayed beyond the stated window, contact jaurastore@gmail.com and we will follow it up with the dispatch partner.
