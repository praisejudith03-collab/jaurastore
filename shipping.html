Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Where we deliver

  • Lagos (Mainland & Island): within 24 to 72 hours.
  • Other Nigerian states & hubs (Ogun, Abuja, Rivers, Edo, Delta, Ekiti, Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Abia, Anambra and more): within 3 to 7 business days.
  • Benin Republic: Cotonou, Abomey-Calavi, Porto-Novo and surrounding areas — within 4 to 12 business days.
  • Togo: Lomé and selected West African destinations — within 4 to 12 business days.

Transport fare

Fares depend on your delivery city and the weight of the parcel. The exact fare is confirmed with you after your order is placed, before dispatch.

Tracking

Once your order is confirmed you will receive an order ID. Message us on WhatsApp with that order ID for a status update.

Delivery issues

If a parcel is delayed beyond the stated window, contact jaurastore@gmail.com and we will follow it up with the dispatch partner.
