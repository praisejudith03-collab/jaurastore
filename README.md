# J Aura Store

Luxury boutique site for Goodness & Praise Okorafor.

- Shop 200+ products across 15 categories
- Customers switch **F CFA** or **₦** and pay in either currency
- WhatsApp checkout (same flow as the original house)
- **Atelier** (`admin.html`) to add, edit, delete and CSV-import products
- Photos you upload are stored exactly as sent — nothing is rewritten

## Atelier login
Pin: `jaura2026`  
Change it under Atelier → Settings.

## Bulk upload
Download the CSV template from Atelier → Bulk upload.  
Required columns: `name, category, priceCfa, priceNgn`.  
Then edit any piece to attach its original photo.
